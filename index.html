Real NFL Intelligence

12-Team Snap & Production Breakdown

How the Week 1 rosters actually performed based on usage, touches, and bench errors.

Honey Bijan Chicken

146.70 Points (1-0)

Dominated the week. Kenneth Walker III (38.6) and Lamar Jackson (32.2) combined for 70+ points. Walker feasted in the red zone to easily command the league's high score.

Delhi Belly

137.10 Points (1-0)

D'Andre Swift exploded for 32.9 points, paired with Javonte Williams (21.7). Add in 21 points from the Jags D/ST, and Rohan easily cleared the defending champ.

Roddy Riccchh

135.75 Points (1-0)

Caleb Williams went absolutely ballistic for 40.95 points. With Justin Jefferson (27.2) and Chris Olave (23.2) vacuuming up targets, this WR/QB stack looks incredibly dangerous.

Quarterback Sackgood

123.20 Points (1-0)

Josh Allen (41.0) and Derrick Henry (35.8) are a bruising two-headed monster. Bagwell survived despite leaving a massive 29.8 points from Jalen Coker stranded on the bench.

Darren's Driven Team

119.90 Points (1-0)

Darren's debut was flawless. David Montgomery (27.4) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (23.7) capitalized on heavy Lion snap counts, while Isaiah Likely (22.8) proved to be an elite TE play.

McDonkey Bhalls

119.15 Points (0-1)

Heartbreak for Jeffrey. Jahmyr Gibbs (31.1) and Breece Hall (19.8) proved to be the best RB duo in the league, but A.J. Brown put up a zero and Matt Stafford struggled against the rush.

Let HIM Cook

115.25 Points (1-0)

Trevor Lawrence found his rhythm (32.05), and Zay Flowers (23.5) was the primary read all day. A 20-point showing from the Seahawks D/ST sealed the win over the LM.

Phil ntheblanks

112.45 Points (0-1)

Ashton Jeanty (30.7) looked like a generational rookie running back. However, Phil's WRs lacked separation, and he left Baker Mayfield's 19.3 point outing rotting on the pine.

Won't You Be My Nabers?

110.55 Points (0-1)

A sluggish start for the preseason #1. CMC was severely limited (9.9). Sean Kramer's real blunder was benching Jameson Williams (26.15) and J.K. Dobbins (22.2).

-- Eating W's

104.50 Points (0-1)

The champ is mortal. Zero-RB hurt immediately as Quinshon Judkins (6.0) and MarShawn Lloyd (3.7) were non-factors. A 26-point Steelers D/ST was the only saving grace.

Saquon my Dak

95.85 Points (0-1)

Spencer's veterans looked old. Davante Adams (4.1) barely saw targets, and the Chargers D/ST (0.0) collapsed. Jonathan Taylor (22.6) was the lone bright spot as Saquon was held to 8.5.

Make Arlington Great Again

78.60 Points (0-1)

Patrick Mahomes dominated and carried this squad, but he was a one-man army. Garrett Ketchen's roster completely imploded around him: Puka Nacua (10.4), Harold Fannin (8.6), Tre Tucker (3.1), and Browns D/ST (0.0).
