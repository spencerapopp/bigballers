Real NFL Intelligence

12-Team Snap & Production Audit

Verified Week 1 performance breakdowns across all twelve league rosters.

Honey Bijan Chicken

146.70 Points (1-0) • Manager: David Mitchell

Pure offensive clinic. Kenneth Walker III (38.60) owned high-value red zone touches while Lamar Jackson (32.20) torched through both the air and ground to easily command the top score.

Delhi Belly

137.10 Points (1-0) • Manager: Rohan Sarode

Committee masterpiece. D'Andre Swift turned in an explosive 32.90 points, Javonte Williams contributed 21.70, and the defense posted 21.0 to dethrone the reigning back-to-back champ.

Roddy Riccchh

135.75 Points (1-0) • Manager: Rodrick Johnson

Caleb Williams exploded for 40.95 points. With Justin Jefferson (27.20) commanding elite target shares, Rodrick proved his wideout-heavy strategy has terrifying weekly upside.

Quarterback Sackgood

123.20 Points (1-0) • Manager: Jonathan Bagwell

Josh Allen (41.00) and Derrick Henry (35.80) carried 76.8 total points by themselves. Survived leaving Jalen Coker's unexpected 29.80-point boom stranded on the bench.

Darren's Driven Team

119.90 Points (1-0) • Manager: Darren Deng

Efficient debut. David Montgomery (27.40) capitalized on heavy inside work, Amon-Ra St. Brown (23.70) converted high-volume chains, and Isaiah Likely (22.80) yielded elite TE scoring.

McDonkey Bhalls

119.15 Points (0-1) • Manager: Jeffrey Bonifield

Cruel bad beat. Jahmyr Gibbs (31.10) and Breece Hall (19.80) demonstrated why they are the most formidable RB room in the league, but Matthew Stafford struggled against intense pressure.

Let HIM Cook

115.25 Points (1-0) • Manager: Kyle Henderson

Trevor Lawrence was sharp (32.05) and Zay Flowers (23.50) dominated underneath separation. Coupled with a 20.0-point showing from Seattle D/ST, Kyle edged past Spencer.

Phil ntheblanks

112.45 Points (0-1) • Manager: Phil Wilkinson

Rookie Ashton Jeanty was electric (30.70) in his debut, backing up the Round 1 capital. However, Phil's perimeter wideouts struggled to separate, coming up 10.75 points short.

Won't You Be My Nabers?

110.55 Points (0-1) • Manager: Sean Kramer

Kramer made the correct coaching calls on the bench: J.K. Dobbins (3.6) and Jameson Williams (6.5) both had quiet days. The problem was his starting core stalling, led by CMC being held to 9.9 points.

-- Eating W's

104.50 Points (0-1) • Manager: Jeff Verber

The defending champion was grounded. Quinshon Judkins (6.0) and MarShawn Lloyd (3.7) were non-factors, leaving a 26.0-point Pittsburgh D/ST game as his only real lifeline.

Saquon my Dak

95.85 Points (0-1) • Manager: Spencer Popp (LM)

Opening-week stall out. Jonathan Taylor (22.60) was steady, but Davante Adams (4.1) disappeared, Saquon was bottle-necked at 8.5, and the Chargers D/ST posted a zero.

Make Arlington Great Again

78.60 Points (0-1) • Manager: Garrett Ketchen

Patrick Mahomes played and dominated, giving Garrett elite QB play. But the rest of the lineup vanished: Browns D/ST put up 0.0, Tre Tucker had 3.1, and Harold Fannin managed 8.6.
